What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include understanding natural language, recognizing patterns, making decisions, and solving problems.

Key Concepts:

  1. Machine Learning: Systems that learn from data
  2. Natural Language Processing: Understanding human language
  3. Computer Vision: Interpreting visual information
  4. Neural Networks: Systems inspired by the human brain
